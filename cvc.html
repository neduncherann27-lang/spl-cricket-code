About CVC

cricket version campos (CVC) is a Stuttgart Premier League member club built around year-round coaching, weekend fixtures and an open door for new players of every level.

From nets on weekday evenings to competitive T10 weekends, CVC keeps the game accessible, friendly and seriously competitive when it matters.
